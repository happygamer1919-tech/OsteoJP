## Why an ALTER POLICY, and why the policy count must NOT move

`attachments_patient_selfscope` (0010) is the portal's read policy. 0089 adds one
conjunct, `deleted_at IS NULL`, by rewriting the whole expression, because
`ALTER POLICY ... USING` replaces it entirely; the two existing conjuncts are
restated character-identically. The role (`patient`) and the command (`SELECT`) are
not changed by that form.

**This is the opposite of 0088's shape and the post-check says so.** 0088 added a
policy and asserted the count went up by one. 0089 rewrites one, so verdict 9
asserts the count is UNCHANGED. A count that grew would mean a second policy was
created, and a PERMISSIVE second policy is ORed — it would give back exactly the
visibility this migration exists to remove.

The staff policy `attachments_tenant_isolation` (0001, FOR ALL, tenant only) is not
touched: verdict 8 pins both halves by md5. A soft-deleted row stays readable to
staff at the database, which is what lets the trail be read.

## The pin is the CONTENT, not a commit sha

Each stage derives the head from `origin/main` and asserts the sha256 of every file
it runs. A rebase or a DECISIONS sync that leaves the three files unchanged proceeds;
one changed byte halts.

## Why two stages, and what each one refuses

**SR-58.** Every stage fetches, re-checks out its own ref detached, and asserts its
files on disk by sha256 before anything runs.

**SR-59.** The post-check's three carries (`journal_rows_before`,
`attachments_policies_before`, `secdef_functions_before`) come from this sitting's
pre-check transcript. Stage 2 parses them out of the file stage 1 wrote and refuses a
transcript older than 60 minutes. No carry name is a substring of another.

The 0084 standard, kept whole: `set -eo pipefail` with psql's stderr in the
transcript; stale transcripts and the marker deleted first, and stage 2 refuses
without a fresh `/tmp/0089-applied.ok`; a verdict matched in its column and the OKs
counted (16 pre, 12 post), each count ending `|| true` so a zero count reaches the
refusal instead of killing the subshell.

**These blocks are pasted into an INTERACTIVE zsh, and that is stricter than a
script.** So: no backslash continuations; every parameter braced, including before a
colon (`${PIN}`, never `$PIN:`); **no `#` comment lines inside the blocks**, because
interactive zsh has `interactivecomments` off and a comment line runs as a command;
and no `!` anywhere, because it is history expansion even inside quotes. Narration is
done with `echo`, which is a command in every shell.
